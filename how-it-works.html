The process

A clear process, from idea to handover.

You work directly with the founder, keeping communication straightforward and decisions close to the project.

Six steps

What working with PeakCraft looks like

1
Initial conversation

Share your business, the problem you want to solve, your priorities and any budget range. No technical jargon required — describe it in your own words.

2
Requirements & scope

We clarify the deliverables, requirements, costs, milestones and what is outside the initial scope. You receive a written scope before work begins.

3
Design & build

Work proceeds against the agreed plan. Progress updates, milestone reviews and the revision allowance are handled as agreed in writing.

4
Review

You review the work against the agreed scope. Feedback within the agreed revision allowance is incorporated; anything extra is quoted separately.

5
Handover

Completed deliverables, access details and relevant documentation are handed over so you know exactly what you have and how to use it.

6
Optional support

If agreed, maintenance can continue on a defined recurring scope. Out-of-scope requests are always quoted before work starts.

What you can expect

Honest communication, no surprises

Written scope

Every project includes a written scope covering what is being delivered, what it costs, and what is not included. This protects both sides.

Honest timelines

Timelines are estimated based on the agreed scope. If something changes, we discuss it and adjust the scope accordingly.

Clear revision allowance

The number of included revisions is agreed in advance. Extra rounds or changes outside scope are quoted separately.

Real handover

You receive what was agreed, with any relevant credentials and documentation. The goal is for you to be able to use and continue with the deliverables.
